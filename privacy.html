Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

KiteWise compares this week's specials at New Zealand's three supermarkets (Woolworths, New World and PAK'nSAVE). This page explains in plain words what we collect, where it's kept, what it's for and how to delete it.

Using it without an account

You can use every price-comparison feature without signing in. The stores you pick and your shopping list stay in your phone's browser. We can't see them.

The app also keeps a few things in your browser so it opens faster, like this week's specials and your language. We don't use any tracking or analytics tools.

When you sign in

You can sign in with your Google or Facebook account. All we get is your name, email address and profile picture.

We never see your password. We can't post for you or see your posts or friends.

What we keep after you sign in

Once you sign in, these are kept in our database (Supabase) so they follow you to a new phone:

  • The stores you picked
  • Your shopping list
  • Items you follow
  • Your push notification subscription (only if you turn notifications on)
  • Your AI recipe history (up to 10; the oldest is removed automatically)
  • Loyalty card numbers (only if you save them; we keep the number only, never a photo)

What we use it for

Only to run the app: comparing prices, syncing your list and sending you notifications.

We don't sell your data or hand it to anyone for their own use (the services below only process it for us). There are no ads right now; if we ever add them, we'll update this page first.

Daily limits

Live price checks and AI recipes have daily limits. To count them we keep a small record:

  • Live price checks: a random device code (stored in your browser) and which products were checked, including names you typed in. This is deleted automatically after about a day.
  • AI recipes: how many each account and each IP address used per day. Counts only, no content, and rows older than 30 days are deleted automatically.

Services we use

These services handle data for us, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in
  • Google and Facebook: whichever one you choose to sign in with
  • OpenAI: writes AI recipes. We send only the ingredients you picked, the preferences you filled in and this week's specials at your stores, never your name, email or account.
  • Your phone's or browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla): delivers notifications
  • Our web host (Zeabur), Google Fonts and the supermarkets' websites (product photos) see your IP address, like any website you visit.

Supermarket prices

Prices come from the supermarkets' public websites and contain no personal information.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time under Me → Delete account. Everything listed above is deleted straight away and can't be recovered. See Deleting your data for the steps.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@kitewise.co.nz.

隱私權政策

最後更新：2026-09-30

KiteWise 幫你比紐西蘭三家超市（Woolworths、New World、PAK'nSAVE）這週的特價。這份說明用白話講清楚：我們拿了你什麼資料、放在哪裡、拿來做什麼、怎麼刪掉。

不登入也能用

不用帳號就能用全部的比價功能。你選的店和購物清單只存在你自己手機的瀏覽器裡，我們拿不到。

App 也會在你的瀏覽器裡存一些東西讓它開得比較快，例如這週的特價和語言設定。我們沒有裝任何追蹤或分析工具。

登入時我們拿到什麼

你可以用 Google 或 Facebook 帳號登入。我們只拿到你的名字、email 和大頭貼。

我們拿不到你的密碼，不能替你發文，也看不到你的貼文或朋友。

登入後存在我們這裡的東西

登入後，下面這些會存在我們的資料庫（Supabase），換手機也還在：

  • 你選的店
  • 購物清單
  • 關注的商品
  • 推播訂閱（你有開推播才有）
  • AI 食譜紀錄（最多 10 筆，滿了自動刪最舊的）
  • 會員卡號碼（你有存才有；只存號碼，不存照片）

拿來做什麼

只用來提供比價、清單同步和通知。

我們不賣你的資料，也不會交給別人拿去用（下面「我們用到的服務」只是替我們處理）。目前沒有廣告；將來如果要加，會先更新這份說明。

每天的次數上限

即時查價和 AI 食譜每天有次數上限，為了算次數會留一點紀錄：

  • 即時查價：一組隨機的裝置代號（存在你的瀏覽器裡）和查了哪些商品（包括你打字搜尋的品名），大約一天後自動刪掉。
  • AI 食譜：每個帳號、每個 IP 位址每天用了幾次。只有次數，沒有內容，超過 30 天的紀錄會自動刪掉。

我們用到的服務

下面這些服務只替我們處理資料，讓 App 跑得起來：

  • Supabase：資料庫和登入
  • Google、Facebook：你選哪一個，就用哪一個登入
  • OpenAI：幫你寫 AI 食譜。只送你勾的食材、你填的偏好和你的店這週的特價，不送你的名字、email 或帳號。
  • 手機或瀏覽器的推播服務（Apple、Google 或 Mozilla）：把通知送到你手上
  • 網站主機（Zeabur）、字型（Google Fonts）和各超市網站（商品照片）：跟你上任何網站一樣，會看到你的 IP 位址。

超市價格

價格是從各超市的公開網站抓的，不含任何個人資料。

刪除帳號

隨時可以在「我的 → 刪除帳號」把帳號整個刪掉。上面列的資料會馬上全部刪除，不能復原。步驟見「刪除資料說明」。
