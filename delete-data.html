Deleting your data

Last updated: 2026-09-30

If you've signed in: delete it in the app

1. Open KiteWise and sign in with the same Google or Facebook account you used before.

2. Tap Me at the bottom, scroll to the end and tap Delete account (in red).

3. Read the note and tap Yes, delete my account.

What gets deleted

Your account (name, email and profile picture) and everything saved with it:

  • The stores you picked
  • Your shopping list (shared list links stop working)
  • Items you follow
  • Push notification subscriptions
  • AI recipe history
  • Loyalty card numbers

The stores and list saved on that phone are cleared too.

How long it takes

It happens straight away and can't be undone. If you sign in with the same Google or Facebook account later, you start again with a new, empty account.

If you never signed in

A guest's stores and list live only on your phone; we don't have them. To clear everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. To just change stores, go to Me → My stores and pick again; to empty your list, tap Clear all on the List page.

Want it deleted without signing in?

Email hello@kitewise.co.nz and include the email address you signed in with (the one on your Google or Facebook account). We'll delete your account for you.

刪除資料說明

最後更新：2026-09-30

登入過的人：在 App 裡刪

1. 打開 KiteWise，用你當初的 Google 或 Facebook 帳號登入。

2. 點下方的「我的」，拉到最下面，點紅字「刪除帳號」。

3. 看完說明，按「我確定要刪除」。

會刪掉什麼

你的帳號（名字、email、大頭貼），和存在帳號裡的所有東西：

  • 你選的店
  • 購物清單（分享出去的清單連結也會失效）
  • 關注的商品
  • 推播訂閱
  • AI 食譜紀錄
  • 會員卡號碼

那支手機上存的店和清單也會一起清掉。

要多久

按下去馬上刪除，不能復原。之後用同一個 Google 或 Facebook 帳號再登入，會是一個全新的空帳號。

沒登入過（訪客）

訪客的店和清單只存在你的手機裡，我們這邊沒有。要全部清掉：到瀏覽器設定清除這個網站的資料。只想換店：到「我的 → 我的店」重新選；清單可以在清單頁按「清空」。

不想登入也想刪

寫信到 hello@kitewise.co.nz，附上你登入用的 email（Google 或 Facebook 帳號的 email），我們會幫你刪掉。
